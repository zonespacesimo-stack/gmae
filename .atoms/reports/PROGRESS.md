# Requirements & Progress

## Requirements Overview
Build a complete HTML5 hyper-casual physics game "One Shot Challenge" with Phaser 3, featuring drag-to-aim mechanics, procedural levels, progression system, 3 game modes, premium game feel, and monetization/analytics hooks.

## User Stories
- As a player, I can drag to aim and release to shoot one projectile per level
- As a player, I see a trajectory preview while aiming
- As a player, I experience satisfying visual/audio feedback on hits and misses
- As a player, I progress through increasingly difficult procedural levels
- As a player, I earn coins and unlock skins
- As a player, I can play Classic, Endless, or Daily Challenge modes
